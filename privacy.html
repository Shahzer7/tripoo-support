Privacy policy

Wayfarer works as a guest. This policy describes how the iPhone app handles information when you browse destinations, save trips, or choose to sign in.

Trips and reminders

Saved itineraries and reminder schedules are stored on your device. Wayfarer does not sync saved trips to a Wayfarer server. Travel reminders are scheduled on the device after you opt in.

Destination searches

Wayfarer uses Apple Maps to look up destinations, activities, and map imagery. Apple handles these requests under its Maps privacy notice.

Optional sign-in

Apple or Google handles authentication when you choose that provider. Wayfarer stores the Apple user identifier or lets the Google Sign-In SDK maintain the sign-in session on your device. Wayfarer uses that state to recognize your sign-in. See the Apple privacy policy and Google privacy policy for how those providers handle their services.

Retention and deletion

Saved trips stay on your device until you remove them. To remove all saved trips and local sign-in data and cancel reminders, open Wayfarer Settings and choose Delete account and app data or Delete Wayfarer data. You can also manage Wayfarer’s access in your Apple or Google account settings.
