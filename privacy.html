Privacy policy

Updated 22 November 2026. Optional account and Travel Buddy features apply in versions that provide them.

This policy covers the published planner and optional account/social features in versions that provide them. Tripoo lets you plan and save travel itineraries. You can use the app without creating an account.

Saved trips and accounts

When you use Tripoo without signing in, your saved trips stay on your iPhone. If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in identifier, the email address supplied by the provider (including an Apple private relay address), and any name you share. Tripoo does not receive your Apple or Google password. Email/password accounts are also available. Firebase Authentication processes the email address and password for account creation and sign-in, and sends password-reset emails when requested. Tripoo sends passwords securely to Firebase Authentication; passwords are never stored in itineraries, Firestore, analytics or GitHub. Email-account ownership is not presented as verified identity.

While signed in, saved itineraries are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and synced across devices using the same account. The saved data includes destination details, trip length, optional travel dates, activity place references, your stop order/day assignments, optional daily notes, and the time the trip was saved. Trips previously saved as a guest are only uploaded if you select “Add trips saved on this iPhone.”

The app uses this data to authenticate your account, save and sync trips, and support account deletion. Apple, Google, and Firebase also process connection and security information as described in their privacy policies. Google Sign-In may use an IP address to estimate a general location for fraud prevention. Provider privacy notices also cover technical identifiers, app/device information, usage information and diagnostics they may process for authentication, security and service operation. Tripoo does not use your account or saved trips for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Optional Travel Buddy

Travel Buddy requires signing in, confirming you are at least 18, and accepting the community standards. Your nickname, selected destination, travel dates, trip length, and introduction are shared with eligible signed-in users only when you publish a buddy listing. Your sign-in email is not included in that listing. You can separately choose to publish an optional short bio and languages in your traveler profile. These details are visible to eligible Travel Buddy members until you clear them or delete your account. Provider photos, emails and names are not automatically imported into this public profile. Earlier preview versions supported activity posts with venue names and times; that stored content remains subject to hiding and account deletion. Age confirmation is self-reported.

Buddy requests and accepted conversations are stored in Firestore. Join introductions are visible to the request’s recipient. Previously shared meeting-place pins, including venue names and precise coordinates, may remain in message history under the same retention and account-deletion rules as text messages. The app does not collect, stream, or track the device’s current location. Text messages are accessible to conversation participants and authorized service administrators; they are not end-to-end encrypted. Private reading-progress timestamps sync across your signed-in devices to identify conversations with new messages. They are not shown to other travelers as read receipts. The current app does not send background chat notifications. Blocking prevents further contact. Reports include the reported content and relevant account identifiers for safety review. Safety reports are automatically removed after 30 days, or sooner when an account involved in the report is deleted. Private automated cleanup runs hourly. It checks that the Firebase account has actually been deleted before removing associated reports and residual account data. Processing normally completes within 24 hours; contact support if it does not. The operator monitors cleanup failures and can retry the job. No indefinite report retention or automatic legal-hold exception is enabled.

You can hide a listing, block another account, report trip, request, profile or message content, or leave a conversation in the app. Changing a trip's dates or deleting the trip withdraws its published listing. Account deletion removes your buddy profile, listings, requests, and conversations, including messages for both participants. Other people may have independently copied information you shared.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics is optional and off by default. In versions with Settings → Share app usage, you can choose to send content-free feature actions (such as planning, saving, sign-in outcomes, buddy requests and message-send outcomes), broad app-section names, foreground active time, app version/build and a random installation reference to PostHog EU Cloud. This reference groups usage from one app installation; it is not your Firebase account ID and is not joined to your Apple or Google identity. We do not send names, email addresses, destinations, travel dates, current location, itinerary notes, message text, report content, advertising identifiers or device fingerprints to PostHog. No session recording, automatic interaction capture, surveys or cross-company advertising tracking is enabled.

PostHog receives network connection information when analytics requests arrive. Tripoo disables geographic enrichment and supplies a neutral IP value in events. Unsent usage events are held briefly in memory, are not saved to disk, and may be lost offline. Turning sharing off discards unsent events and cancels pending requests; an event already received by PostHog may remain. Deleting a Tripoo account or guest data also turns sharing off on that installation. It does not automatically erase historical installation-based usage, which is separate from account data. PostHog’s free plan retains received analytics for up to one year. To request earlier deletion, email hasannawaz767@gmail.com using the usage reference displayed in Settings → Share app usage before uninstalling the app. The operator uses that reference to locate and delete the corresponding PostHog person and events, and confirms completion. The app’s deletion-request link opens an email draft; you must send it. Deleting an account does not remove analytics or consent settings from other installations.

Sharing an itinerary

Sharing is optional. Tripoo shows a preview and uses the iPhone share sheet to send the text to an app or person you select. You choose whether to include travel dates, itinerary stops and private notes; dates and private notes are excluded by default. Your sign-in email and account identifiers are not added to the shared itinerary. The receiving app or recipient handles any copy you share under its own policies.

Maps and destination content

City search and destination photographs use bundled resources. The app does not contact photo providers to display these photographs. Source and license links open only when you choose them. City search uses a bundled city catalog. Apple Maps supplies activity search results and map content. Tripoo stores Apple place references to retrieve current activity details when a saved trip is reopened. Apple processes map-service requests under its own privacy policy. Selecting a travel destination does not provide Tripoo with your current device location.

Travel reminders

Travel reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. You can disable them in Tripoo's Settings or iOS Settings.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove individual saved trips. Deleting a trip while signed in removes it from your account and syncs that deletion to your devices. Offline changes are queued until a network connection is available.

Use Settings → Account and trip sync → Delete account to delete your Firebase account and synced trips. You will be asked to confirm your identity with Apple or Google, or re-enter your password for an email account. A minimal deletion marker containing the former account identifier remains for at least two hours to prevent stale, still-valid sign-in tokens on offline devices from restoring deleted trips. The hourly cleanup job removes the marker after that grace period and related cleanup, after verifying that the Firebase account is deleted. A failed cleanup retains the protective marker until processing succeeds. Guest trips remain separate; use Delete Tripoo data while signed out to remove those local copies and cancel travel reminders.

Signing out does not delete your cloud trips. Account-specific local caches may be retained to support offline access when you sign back in; they are not shown to a different signed-in account. Other devices clear their account cache when they receive the deletion marker. An offline device must reconnect before it can receive that deletion.

Privacy requests

Contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information, or to ask about safety-report retention. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account before acting on it. Account and synced content are retained until you remove them or delete your account, except the safety reports and minimal deletion markers described above. You can withdraw from optional account features by signing out; signing out alone does not erase stored data.
