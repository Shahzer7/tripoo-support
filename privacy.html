Privacy policy

Updated 21 November 2026. Optional account and Travel Buddy features apply in versions that provide them.

This policy covers the published planner and optional account/social features in versions that provide them. Tripoo lets you plan and save travel itineraries. You can use the app without creating an account.

Saved trips and accounts

When you use Tripoo without signing in, your saved trips stay on your iPhone. If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in identifier, the email address supplied by the provider (including an Apple private relay address), and any name you share. Tripoo does not receive your Apple or Google password.

While signed in, saved itineraries are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and synced across devices using the same account. The saved data includes destination details, trip length, optional travel dates, activity place references, your stop order/day assignments, optional daily notes, and the time the trip was saved. Trips previously saved as a guest are only uploaded if you select “Add trips saved on this iPhone.”

The app uses this data to authenticate your account, save and sync trips, and support account deletion. Apple, Google, and Firebase also process connection and security information as described in their privacy policies. Google Sign-In may use an IP address to estimate a general location for fraud prevention. Provider privacy notices also cover technical identifiers, app/device information, usage information and diagnostics they may process for authentication, security and service operation. Tripoo does not use your account or saved trips for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Optional Travel Buddy

Travel Buddy requires signing in, confirming you are at least 18, and accepting the community standards. Your nickname, selected destination, travel dates, trip length, and introduction are shared with eligible signed-in users only when you publish a buddy listing. Your sign-in email is not included in that listing. You can separately choose to publish an optional short bio and languages in your traveler profile. These details are visible to eligible Travel Buddy members until you clear them or delete your account. Provider photos, emails and names are not automatically imported into this public profile. Earlier preview versions supported activity posts with venue names and times; that stored content remains subject to hiding and account deletion. Age confirmation is self-reported.

Buddy requests and accepted conversations are stored in Firestore. Join introductions are visible to the request’s recipient. Previously shared meeting-place pins, including venue names and precise coordinates, may remain in message history under the same retention and account-deletion rules as text messages. The app does not collect, stream, or track the device’s current location. Text messages are accessible to conversation participants and authorized service administrators; they are not end-to-end encrypted. Private reading-progress timestamps sync across your signed-in devices to identify conversations with new messages. They are not shown to other travelers as read receipts. The current app does not send background chat notifications. Blocking prevents further contact. Reports include the reported content and relevant account identifiers for safety review. Reports are retained separately while needed to investigate abuse, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations, and are not automatically removed by account deletion. Contact support about report retention or privacy requests.

You can hide a listing, block another account, report trip, request, profile or message content, or leave a conversation in the app. Changing a trip's dates or deleting the trip withdraws its published listing. Account deletion removes your buddy profile, listings, requests, and conversations, including messages for both participants. Other people may have independently copied information you shared.

Sharing an itinerary

Sharing is optional. Tripoo shows a preview and uses the iPhone share sheet to send the text to an app or person you select. You choose whether to include travel dates, itinerary stops and private notes; dates and private notes are excluded by default. Your sign-in email and account identifiers are not added to the shared itinerary. The receiving app or recipient handles any copy you share under its own policies.

Maps and destination content

City search and destination photographs use bundled resources. The app does not contact photo providers to display these photographs. Source and license links open only when you choose them. City search uses a bundled city catalog. Apple Maps supplies activity search results and map content. Tripoo stores Apple place references to retrieve current activity details when a saved trip is reopened. Apple processes map-service requests under its own privacy policy. Selecting a travel destination does not provide Tripoo with your current device location.

Travel reminders

Travel reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. You can disable them in Tripoo's Settings or iOS Settings.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove individual saved trips. Deleting a trip while signed in removes it from your account and syncs that deletion to your devices. Offline changes are queued until a network connection is available.

Use Settings → Account and trip sync → Delete account to delete your Firebase account and synced trips. You will be asked to confirm your identity with Apple or Google. A minimal deletion marker containing the former account identifier is retained to prevent offline devices from restoring deleted trips. Guest trips remain separate; use Delete Tripoo data while signed out to remove those local copies and cancel travel reminders.

Signing out does not delete your cloud trips. Account-specific local caches may be retained to support offline access when you sign back in; they are not shown to a different signed-in account. Other devices clear their account cache when they receive the deletion marker. An offline device must reconnect before it can receive that deletion.

Privacy requests

Contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information, or to ask about safety-report retention. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account before acting on it. Account and synced content are retained until you remove them or delete your account, except the safety reports and minimal deletion markers described above. You can withdraw from optional account features by signing out; signing out alone does not erase stored data.
