Support

Get help with Tripoo, an iPhone app for exploring destinations and building travel itineraries.

Contact support

Email hasannawaz767@gmail.com for help, feedback, or a privacy question. If you are reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Plan a trip

Choose a country, city, and duration in Explore, then tap Build my itinerary. Save the itinerary to find it again under Saved.

Saved trips and reminders

Tripoo works without an account. You can delete your saved trips from Settings. Travel reminders are optional and can be turned off in Settings.
