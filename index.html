Support

Get help with Tripoo, an iPhone app for exploring destinations and building travel itineraries.

Contact support

Email hasannawaz767@gmail.com for help, feedback, or a privacy question. If you are reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Plan a trip

Choose a country, city, and duration in Explore, then tap Build my itinerary. Save the itinerary to find it again under Saved. When suggestions are unavailable, choose Plan it myself to start editable days and add private daily notes; searching for places still needs an internet connection.

Saved stops and connection problems

Saved trip dates, day structure and notes can open on your iPhone while Apple Maps place details load. Details are retrieved for the day you open. Use Retry saved stops when needed. Saved shows your sync status; Retry sync reconnects after a sync error. Travel Buddy publishing, requests and chat need a connection. If publishing fails after your private trip is saved, the app explains that the trip is not public yet.

Accounts and trip sync

In versions with account features, use Settings → Account and trip sync to sign in with Apple or Google. Signed-in trips sync across devices using the same account. Guest trips are imported only when you choose “Add trips saved on this iPhone.” To erase your account and synced content, choose Delete account and complete provider confirmation.

Travel Buddy and safety

In versions with Travel Buddy, adults can publish a trip or request to join a traveler. Private text chat starts after the host accepts. Use Report or Block on a trip, profile, request or message for unwanted content or contact. Email support for help with a report, account restriction or privacy request. Do not email passwords or other sensitive account credentials. Tripoo does not verify identities or provide emergency response.

Destination galleries

Choose a country in Explore to see available destination photos, or select All galleries. Photo availability varies by country; the app does not yet provide five or six photos for every country. Source and license links appear in Photo credits.

Saved trips and reminders

Tripoo works without an account. Remove individual trips under Saved, or use Delete Tripoo data in Settings for local guest data. Signing out does not delete cloud trips. Travel reminders are optional and can be turned off in Settings.
